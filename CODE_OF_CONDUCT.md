# Code of conduct

We want the ASC-IT and Darkmoon communities to be welcoming to everyone who wants to build, test, break and improve security tooling together.

**We expect participants to:**

- be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews,
- welcome newcomers and assume good faith,
- accept feedback on their work and give it with care,
- use Darkmoon and the other projects only against systems they are authorized to test.

**We do not tolerate:**

- harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks,
- publishing other people's private information,
- using our projects or channels to attack systems without authorization.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that break these rules, and may ban repeat offenders.

Report a problem to **support@asc-it.fr**. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
